My contribution to the KV4 project – Vacuum Chamber

The KV4 chamber was designed to impregnate mushrooms with water or preserving solutions according to specified process parameters.

The starting point was an existing chamber design with capacity for 2 pallets. My task was to develop a new version for 4 pallets while meeting the spatial and process requirements.

As part of the project, I:

  • recalculated the tank volume according to the customer’s requirements,
  • selected pumps with sufficient capacity to achieve the required cycle time,
  • developed a design with an unconventional component layout to accommodate the limited space at the plant.

KV4 was a significant engineering challenge, requiring close coordination between mechanical design and process parameters.
