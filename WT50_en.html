Renders • Engineering solutions

My contribution to the project WT50 – Waterfall

WT50 began as a modernization of an existing machine, but the scope of the changes ultimately led to a complete design from scratch. The machine orients jar lids and feeds them in the required position.

I developed solutions for adapting the machine to different lid widths and diameters and for quickly changing between production recipes.

  • An adjustment mechanism for different lid widths and diameters.
  • An automatic inclination adjustment mechanism.
  • A concept for quick changeovers between production recipes.
  • Interchangeable format parts for switching to a different lid format.
